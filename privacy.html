Privacy Policy

Witness @ Six / Witness Network

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. About This Application

Witness @ Six is a community news broadcast associated with Witness Network. Its publishing application uses the YouTube Data API to manage uploads of its own nightly video broadcasts to an authorized YouTube channel.

2. Google and YouTube Permissions

The application requests Google OAuth authorization for YouTube upload and read-only channel access. These permissions are used to:

  • Identify the YouTube channel that authorized access.
  • Upload Witness @ Six video broadcasts.
  • Read relevant channel information for verification.

The application does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or Google account passwords.

3. Information We Process

The application may process authorized YouTube channel identifiers, channel names, OAuth access and refresh tokens, upload status, video identifiers, and broadcast metadata necessary for publishing.

Video files and associated metadata are submitted to YouTube when an authorized upload is performed.

4. Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored on the application operator's server for authorized publishing. They are not intended to be published in the public website repository or shared with visitors.

Reasonable access controls are used to restrict access to stored credentials.

5. Sharing of Information

The application sends authorized video uploads and necessary request information to Google's YouTube services. It does not sell Google user data.

Google and YouTube process information according to their own applicable policies.

6. Retention and Deletion

Authorization credentials may be retained while the application requires access to the authorized YouTube channel. Upload records may be retained for operational tracking and duplicate prevention.

An authorized user may request deletion of locally stored authorization information by contacting the application operator.

7. Revoking Google Access

Users can review and revoke the application's Google account permissions through:

Google Account — Third-Party Connections

Revoking access prevents future authorized API operations unless permission is granted again. Local stored credentials may also need to be deleted by the application operator.

8. YouTube Terms and Google Privacy

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube-related functionality, users are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service .

Google's Privacy Policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy .

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated if the application changes its permissions, publishing features, or data-handling practices. Updates will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or requests involving authorization data, contact the Witness Network application operator through the official Witness Network community channels.
